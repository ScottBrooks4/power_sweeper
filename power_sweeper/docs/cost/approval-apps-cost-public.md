# These are estimates with citations.

Annual cost of a ten-person team at Carling Campus building approval apps for the Vice Chief of the Defence Staff.

At about 10,500 openers, under the licensing guide, the feasible stacks that are not a per-person Power Apps licence are: Custom web, SQL, Azure Canada; Custom web, PostgreSQL, AWS Canada (Central); Custom web, XML LOM, Azure Canada; DWAN virtual machine. They sit inside the salary-step noise ($58,972). There is no single cheapest host. Full M365 with SharePoint lists is where the team is, and a year of submissions at that size fails the SharePoint 5,000-item view threshold (not the Power Apps 500 / 2,000 delegation limit).

## Team

Salary at step 4: $1,028,060. Loaded at 1.27: $1,305,636. Mix: 1 IT-04, 3 IT-03, 6 IT-02, step 4, rates of 22 December 2024. Exchange rate 1.4145 USD/CAD on 2026-09-25.

## Cost-effectiveness to 2030

Addressable remaining mid backlog about 165 VCDS approval-style projects by 2030 (87–254). Historical pace is 4.3 a year from 30 projects in 7 years, costed at an average 4.5 people over the last five years, not at today's ten. On VCR, assisted hours changed YAML about 5.2× faster than a person-hour in the six weeks you and a student built the body of the app. Ten people at that pace do not clear the mid backlog. If tools improve as projected, those ten land about 82 projects by 2030, and 21 builders clear the mid line. Legacy apps are converted with Power sweeper (18 person-weeks), not hand-ported (200 person-weeks).

Sixteen VCDS organizations (January 2026 welcome package) times 6–14 digitizable approval or admin processes each, plus 12–30 pan-VCDS workflows. Subtract the 30 already done. Add a refresh of about half of those for 2030. DRMIS, classified C2, and pure records systems are out of scope. Author planning ranges, not a census.

## Build

- Replace lists only. Screens stay in Power Apps. Mid 12 person-weeks, one-time $30,028, licence avoided $0 a year at 10,500 openers. does not pay back on licences. The custom or premium connector remains. Under the licensing guide the per-opener seat does not go away. The build pays only if the 5,000-item view was the reason.
- Replace Power Automate approvals only. Mid 14 person-weeks, one-time $35,033, licence avoided $8,487 a year at 10,500 openers. payback about 50.5 months including 1 months of calendar before savings. A per-flow licence covers the flow, not each person who opens an app. The minimum published purchase is five flows.
- Replace screens and lists with a web app on SQL. Mid 48 person-weeks, one-time $120,114, licence avoided $3,562,458 a year at 10,500 openers. payback about 12.4 months including 12 months of calendar before savings. This is the build that drops the per-opener Power Apps seat. Later apps reuse the shell.
- Replace screens and lists with a web app on LOM. Mid 36 person-weeks, one-time $90,085, licence avoided $3,567,054 a year at 10,500 openers. payback about 12.3 months including 12 months of calendar before savings. Same seat drop, shorter weeks because the engine already exists. Still a new authorization. One document is the wrong shape at this size; the weeks assume the documents are partitioned.
- Do not buy Fabric. Stand up SQL. Mid 4 person-weeks, one-time $10,009, licence avoided $4,956 a year at 10,500 openers. payback about 25.2 months including 1 months of calendar before savings. Fabric is treated as a purchase not yet made. The avoided bill is the F2 capacity, not a migration.
