VCDS Group OHS Risk Assessment

Four Power Apps canvas mockups at 1366×768. Use the PNG files in backgrounds/ as screen images, then overlay galleries, buttons and forms. Sample data is from the Aug 2026 L2 register (59 records).

1 · Dashboard — leadership risk picture

Filters by L2, program and risk level. Heat map is probability × severity from the register. WHMIS pictograms appear on chemical / explosives / health-hazard rows.

2 · Inbox — new risk identified

Role-based landing after sign-in. OHS officers are notified of new or reopened records. Identify-risk panel on the right.

3 · Review — score, contact, RACI

Featured record: CFSG(O-G) RA-0007 Hazardous Substances. L2 vs L1 scoring, contact Luc Joly, adjust or accept score.

4 · Treat — mitigate, evidence, close

Hierarchy of controls, evidence-of-compliance checklist, residual risk acceptance, archive / active monitoring.
